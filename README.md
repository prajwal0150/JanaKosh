## About

**Janakosh** (जनकोश) is an open-source civic technology platform that makes 
Nepal's government budget and tax data transparent, visual, and accessible to 
every citizen.

The name comes from two Nepali words — *Jana* (जन, people) and *Kosh* (कोश, 
treasury) — meaning **"The People's Treasury."**

Every year, the Nepal government allocates billions of rupees to 7 provinces, 
77 districts, and 753 local government units. Citizens pay taxes but have no 
simple way to know where that money goes. Janakosh changes that.

### What it does
- 🗺️ Interactive Nepal map — drill down from province → district → municipality
- 💰 Budget tracker — see allocation vs actual spending for every local unit
- 🧾 Tax calculator — estimate your contribution and see where it went
- 🚨 Anomaly alerts — auto-flags municipalities with suspicious unspent budgets
- 📊 Year-on-year charts — track how investment in your area changed over time
- 🇳🇵 Full Nepali language support

### Tech stack
`MongoDB` · `Express.js` · `React.js` · `Node.js` · `MySQL` · `Tailwind CSS`
